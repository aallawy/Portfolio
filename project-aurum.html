Project story

Creating a boutique identity with warmth and restraint

The goal for Aurum was not loud luxury. It was quiet confidence. I shaped the brand around rich burgundy tones, a warm metallic accent, and typography that felt refined without becoming cold or distant.

The final system was designed to move smoothly between in-store materials and digital promotion, giving the brand a single visual voice whether it appeared on a box, a gift card, or a launch post.

Deliverables

Brand assets produced

  • Primary and secondary logo lockups with a monogram mark
  • Color palette, typography pairings, and image direction
  • Packaging concepts for boxes, cards, tags, and inserts
  • Social post templates for campaigns, drops, and promotions
Process

From positioning to rollout

The identity system was built by defining the brand's tone first, then translating that tone into a consistent set of visual decisions across every customer touchpoint.

01

Brand positioning

I started by defining the boutique as curated, elevated, and personal, which set the direction for tone, materials, and visual restraint.

02

Identity building

Logo forms, color, and typography were refined together so the system felt timeless and recognizable across both print and digital applications.

03

Launch applications

The final stage focused on how the brand would appear in motion: packaging reveals, campaign teasers, and social templates designed for repeated use.

6 brand touchpoints designed across print and digital
3 core tones that define the boutique's signature look
1 flexible identity system ready for retail growth
