Project story

Designing a logo system with power, clarity, and presence

I was asked to design a primary logo and a secondary logo for an established company with an existing color palette already in place. The goal was not to rebuild the brand from zero, but to create a stronger identity that felt intentional, elevated, and unmistakably aligned with a woman-led business.

For the primary mark, I used a lioness as the central symbol of strength, protection, leadership, and empowerment. For the secondary logo, I simplified the system into a clean typographic lockup that stays versatile and easy to use across banners, footers, pitch decks, and minimal brand applications.

Deliverables

Brand assets produced

  • Primary lioness logo built within the existing palette
  • Secondary typographic logo for flexible digital use
  • Typography pairing guidance for serif and sans applications
  • Primary and secondary color palette presentation
  • Branded guideline pages to explain the identity system
Identity direction

Built to feel empowered without losing elegance.

The final system balances bold symbolism with cleaner supporting elements so the brand can feel both commanding and polished in modern digital contexts.

01

Respect the existing palette

I worked within the company's established colors first, using them as a foundation instead of forcing a new direction that would disconnect from the brand.

02

Shape the lioness mark

The primary logo was developed around a geometric lioness to express intelligence, resilience, and the protective strength behind a woman-led company.

03

Simplify the secondary logo

The supporting wordmark was kept cleaner and more versatile so it can scale easily across minimal layouts, brand signatures, and repeated everyday use.
