NaturCycle App

A sustainable beauty app concept that brings shopping, skin guidance, and a closed-loop recycling program into one calm mobile experience. I developed the UX research, wireframes, prototype, case-study direction, logo, and brand colors so the whole system would feel natural, elegant, and easy to use.

Client NaturCycle concept brand
Scope UX research, wireframes, prototype, branding
Tools Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop
Project story

Connecting skincare, education, and circular design in one thoughtful experience.

NaturCycle began as a UX case study for a beauty brand that needed to do more than sell products. The app had to help people browse skincare with confidence, understand their own skin, and return empty packaging without the process feeling like a chore.

I built the project around a soft editorial interface where shopping, skin guidance, recycling, and account tasks sit in the same navigation. The structure and the visual identity were worked out together, so the app feels trustworthy, botanical, and quietly premium from the first screen.

Deliverables

What I created for NaturCycle

  • UX research direction centered on sustainable beauty habits, trust, and ease of action
  • 20 mobile screens covering onboarding, shopping and checkout, the skin check, recycling returns, and the account area
  • A clickable Figma prototype linking the main flows, plus screens for errors, empty results, and a denied camera permission
  • A custom logo direction and botanical brand palette woven directly into the interface
Research and process

Designing for users who want cleaner routines and clearer next steps.

The case study focused on cutting friction from eco-conscious shopping: helping people understand products faster, making the brand credible, and putting recycling in the main flow instead of treating it as an afterthought.

01

UX research framing

I mapped the experience around three user needs: confidence in the product, ease while shopping, and clarity around how NaturCycle's return and recycling model actually works.

02

Wireframes and flow design

The app is organized into five areas: home, shop, skin, recycle, and account. Recycling gets its own tab and a full return flow, and each flow includes the screens for when something goes wrong.

03

Brand-led interface

Deep greens, soft cream surfaces, and a restrained type style give the app a natural, elevated feel without costing usability.

Brand identity

A nature-led visual system built to feel polished, soft, and intentional.

The NaturCycle identity was designed to balance sustainability with elegance. Instead of leaning on loud eco cues, I shaped the brand around a calmer palette, softer surfaces, and a more editorial tone that helps the experience feel premium while still rooted in nature.

The logo direction, muted olive actions, creamy panels, and dark forest framing all work together to support the product story. That visual language runs through every screen, so the branding does not sit beside the UX. It becomes part of how the product communicates trust and care.
