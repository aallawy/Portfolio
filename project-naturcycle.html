Project story

Connecting skincare, education, and circular design in one thoughtful experience.

NaturCycle began as a UX case study for a beauty brand that needed to do more than sell products. The experience had to help users browse skincare with confidence, understand what they were putting on their skin, and participate in a recycling or return system without the process feeling complicated.

I shaped the project around a softer editorial interface where shopping, sustainability education, and account-based actions could live inside the same ecosystem. That meant solving both the structural UX work and the visual identity at the same time, so the app could feel trustworthy, botanical, and quietly premium from the very first screen.

Research and process

Designing for users who want cleaner routines and clearer next steps.

The case study focused on reducing friction around eco-conscious shopping: helping users understand products faster, making the brand feel more credible, and connecting the beauty experience to a visible recycling journey instead of treating sustainability like an afterthought.

01

UX research framing

I mapped the experience around three user needs: confidence in the product, ease while shopping, and clarity around how NaturCycle's return and recycling model actually works.

02

Wireframes and flow design

The app structure was built around onboarding, discovery, product review, cart, and account actions, with the recycling program treated as a core journey rather than a buried support page.

03

Brand-led interface polish

The final visual direction combined deep greens, soft cream surfaces, and a refined typographic tone so the app could feel natural and elevated without losing usability.

Brand identity

A nature-led visual system built to feel polished, soft, and intentional.

The NaturCycle identity was designed to balance sustainability with elegance. Instead of leaning on loud eco cues, I shaped the brand around a calmer palette, softer surfaces, and a more editorial tone that helps the experience feel premium while still rooted in nature.

The logo direction, muted olive actions, creamy panels, and dark forest framing all work together to support the product story. That visual language carries through the mockups so the branding does not sit beside the UX. It becomes part of how the product communicates trust and care.
