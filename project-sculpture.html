Project story

Exploring form, balance, and atmosphere in 3D

This series was created as a study in contrast: heavy and light, precise and organic, still and cinematic. I wanted each sculpture to feel like it had been discovered mid-motion, with shape and lighting doing most of the storytelling.

The final direction combined soft volume with a more structured internal geometry. That tension helped each render feel sculptural from a distance while still rewarding a closer look.

Deliverables

What was developed

  • Concept silhouettes and wireframe studies for structure testing
  • High-resolution hero renders for portfolio and exhibition use
  • Multiple lighting passes to shape tone and surface drama
  • Composition exploration for stills, motion, and installation mockups
Process

How the series evolved

The work moved from loose silhouette studies into cleaner forms, with lighting and camera framing used as part of the design rather than a final afterthought.

01

Shape discovery

I experimented with asymmetrical forms and negative space until each composition felt balanced but slightly unstable in an intentional way.

02

Surface refinement

Wireframes helped test where to soften edges, where to compress mass, and how to create cleaner rhythm throughout the models.

03

Cinematic lighting

Final renders were staged with stronger highlights and controlled shadow falloff so each still felt dramatic, intentional, and gallery-ready.
