Day and Night Room Visualization

A 3D interior room project built to simulate a believable real space in two distinct moods: one rendered in daylight and one staged for nighttime.

Project story

Creating a realistic room in two lighting situations

This project focused on building a 3D modeled interior room that could feel believable in two different sittings: one in the daytime and one in the nighttime. The goal was to keep the room itself consistent while letting lighting, atmosphere, and tone shift naturally between the two scenes.

I used Adobe Modeler to shape the room and Adobe Stager to refine the environment, materials, and final rendering. The contrast between the daylight and nighttime versions helped show how the same space can communicate a completely different feeling through light alone.

Deliverables

What was created

  • A fully modeled 3D room environment with realistic layout and styling
  • Two final rendered scenes showing the room in daytime and nighttime
  • Material and lighting adjustments to support believable mood changes
  • Presentation-ready still images for portfolio display
Process

How the room was developed

The workflow centered on building one strong environment first, then adjusting the scene settings so the same room could feel convincing at two different times of day.

01

Room modeling

I built the overall space and furniture arrangement in Adobe Modeler, focusing on a clean structure that could support realistic staging and camera framing.

02

Scene setup

In Adobe Stager I refined the materials, object styling, and environmental setup so the room felt grounded and visually coherent as a real interior.

03

Lighting variation

The final renders explored how daylight and nighttime lighting could change the atmosphere of the exact same room without changing the modeled environment itself.
