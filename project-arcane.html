Project story

Translating a monologue into atmosphere, symbolism, and motion.

This piece was built around a speech from one of the characters in Arcane. Instead of relying on ready-made clips or found imagery, I treated the dialogue as the foundation for a fully animated visual response, using motion and composition to reflect what the speech is really communicating underneath the words.

The goal was to let the animation feel like an extension of the voice itself. Every shape, transition, and movement was created from scratch in After Effects so the piece could carry the tone of the show while still standing as its own visual interpretation.

Focus

What shaped the piece

  • Original animation created entirely from scratch
  • Visual pacing designed around the speech's emotional rhythm
  • Imagery built to represent meaning rather than literal scenes
  • Color and motion chosen to match Arcane's darker atmosphere
  • One cohesive motion study developed in Adobe After Effects
Approach

Built around voice, mood, and visual interpretation.

The piece works less like a literal retelling and more like a visual translation of what the speech is carrying emotionally, using motion to turn tone and subtext into something visible.

01

Study the speech

I broke the monologue down into emotional beats first, focusing on tension, emphasis, and shifts in tone to decide where the animation should hold back or intensify.

02

Design original visuals

The imagery was created from scratch so the visuals could feel tailored to the language and mood instead of depending on ready-made references or clips.

03

Animate the emotion

Movement, transitions, and timing were shaped to act like a visual echo of the speech, giving the piece a sense of buildup, weight, and release.
