Project story

Building an energetic brand world that feels shelf-ready.

Nectar was developed as a hypothetical energy drink company by me and a team, with the brand guidelines book designed to show how the identity could live beyond the logo and into a full visual system.

The direction focused on a louder, more energized tone so the brand would feel attention-grabbing, youthful, and flexible across both print and branded applications. The book moves from introducing the logo to showing how it should be used, then into mockups that make the identity feel real on product and merch.

Deliverables

What the brand book includes

  • Primary brand mark presented within the guidelines system
  • Logo usage examples for print and web contexts
  • Branded layout spreads for the guidelines book
  • Product mockups for the energy drink packaging
  • Merch mockups to extend the identity beyond the can
