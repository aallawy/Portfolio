Forma Magazine

Art direction and layout design for a quarterly design publication, balancing strong visual pacing with an editorial system that stays readable issue after issue.

Client Independent design magazine
Timeline 3 week production sprint
Tools InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator
Read case study Plan an editorial project
Project story

Building an editorial rhythm that feels polished and readable

Forma was designed to feel contemporary without becoming visually noisy. The layout system needed to support long-form reading, spotlight photography, and recurring departments while still giving each spread its own moment.

I focused on hierarchy, white space, and a repeatable grid structure so the magazine could feel art directed at every turn without losing consistency from cover to closing pages.

Deliverables

Editorial assets produced

  • Cover direction with masthead and issue system
  • Feature spreads, department templates, and opener pages
  • Grid framework for text-heavy and image-led content
  • Print-ready production setup for a polished handoff
Process

How the issue was shaped

The design process balanced expression with structure, making sure the publication had enough personality to feel distinctive and enough system to stay scalable.

01

Issue framing

I outlined the pacing of the publication first so the reader would move through quiet pages, bold features, and shorter departments with clear momentum.

02

Grid and hierarchy

The editorial system was built around flexible columns, disciplined spacing, and headline treatments that work across both short and long-form content.

03

Final production

Once the core templates were locked, the remaining pages were refined for consistency, page turns, and print-ready quality.
